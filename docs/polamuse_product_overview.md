# Polamuse
### Turn your photos into polaroids. Design it. Share it. Send it.

---

## What is Polamuse?

Polamuse is a website you open on your phone.

It lets you take any photo and turn it into a beautiful
polaroid — with a frame, a caption, filters, and your style.
You can download it, share it, or order a real physical print
delivered to anyone in India.

No app to download. No design skills needed.
Just open the website and start.

## What can you do on Polamuse?

---

### 1. Design a Polaroid

Open the editor on your phone.
Upload a photo from your gallery or take one with your camera.
Choose a frame style — classic white border, instax mini, movie poster, vintage, and more.

Then make it yours:
- Add a caption at the bottom ("summer '24", "always you", a song lyric)
- Add a name or label at the top
- Apply a filter — vintage film, black & white, faded, sepia
- Adjust brightness, contrast, warmth with simple sliders
- Add stickers, a date stamp, or a Spotify scan code for the song you love
- Change the frame color or texture

When you're done, download the image to your phone for free.

---

### 2. Booth Mode — Phone Photobooth

No photobooth machine needed.

Tap "Booth Mode" → your front camera opens →
tap once → it automatically takes 4 photos with a countdown.

Done. Your phone just became a photobooth.

The 4 shots are instantly arranged into a film strip — the kind you'd
pull from a real photo booth machine. Download it, send it to friends,
post it on Instagram.

Perfect for farewell parties, college fests, friend trips,
or any moment you want to capture without planning.

---

### 3. Make One Together — Shared Canvas

You're in Nagpur. Your best friend is in Bangalore.

Tap "Make Together" → a link is created → send it on WhatsApp.
Your friend opens it on their phone.

Now you're both looking at the same polaroid.
You add your photo. They add theirs.
You type a caption. They add a sticker.

One polaroid, made together, from two different cities.
Download it or order it printed.

---

### 4. Send It as a Surprise — Gift Mode

You designed something beautiful.
Instead of ordering it to your own address,
tap "Send as a gift."

Enter your friend's address.
Write a message — "Happy birthday yaar, miss you."

We print the polaroid, write your message on a small card,
wrap it in kraft paper, and ship it to their door.

They receive a physical surprise in their mailbox.
No heads up. Just a moment they weren't expecting.
